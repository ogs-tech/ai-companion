# Workspaces

A workspace is a folder, or a group of folders, the app works in. Home (Início) is the place outside any workspace.

Everything else in the app (files, Git, terminals, sessions, customizations) is scoped to the workspace you have open.

- [Manage workspaces](manage-workspaces.md) — create, switch, rename and remove workspaces; what a workspace inherits from Global.
- [Work with multi-folder workspaces](work-with-multi-folder-workspaces.md) — several repositories in one workspace, per-folder customizations, which folders a session sees.
